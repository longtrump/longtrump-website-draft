Long Trump was founded in Taipei. We are an engineering-led distributor of electronic components. For more than twenty years we have done one thing: make sure a customer’s design can actually be built when it is time for volume.

Company film

One minute with Long Trump.

An electronic-component channel partner since 1985 — from Taipei to the Shenzhen and Shanghai sites, the Zhihong Technology battery plant established in 2003, and the FAE and service teams who work through problems with customers every day.

The value of a distributor
shows up the day a part is short.

The lines we represent span wireless, sensing, power semiconductors, optical interfaces, batteries, and long-lifecycle parts. Most customers are global ODMs, OEMs, and brand manufacturers. A request often includes both samples for a new design and service parts for a model from ten years ago.

That mix forces two things to be done well: engineering capability, and supply-chain discipline. The first is why we are brought in at the design stage. The second is why we are still there at volume.

Group sites cover Taipei, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hong Kong, and Japan. Paid-in capital is NT$150 million. The quality system has held ISO 9001 since 2002.

Three unromantic things
that are hard to do.

If a part is a poor fit, we say so

Recommending the wrong part becomes a complaint three years later. In a selection meeting we would rather state the limit first than repair it afterward.

Measure first, then answer

An FAE’s drawer has oscilloscope probes, not only catalogs. If we can verify a symptom ourselves, we do not just forward the question to the factory.

After the contract, the relationship stays

End-of-life parts, service parts, a rush insertion — requests with no revenue number decide whether the customer still calls in ten years.

“The quality policy is only six characters: Learn, Innovate, Perform.
It is audited every year, so it cannot only be written on the wall.” Long Trump quality manual

A few years that left a mark.

  1. 2002

    First ISO 9001 certification. The quality system began to run on documents and audits, not on personal memory.

  2. 2019

    Paid-in capital raised to NT$150 million. A financial base for buffer stock, long agreements, and the overseas setup.

  3. Each year

    Internal audit and management review. Every department sets annual quality objectives. Mid-year internal audit, year-end external follow-up.

  4. 2026

    Passed ISO 9001:2015 recertification. The scope covers sales of semiconductor components, batteries, connectors, antennas, and software.

Behind one product line,
four departments’ ordinary days.

Three business lines, each watching its own factories and customers

BU1 communications, sensing, and power. BU2 batteries and power. BU3 long-lifecycle parts. Each PM is tied to a fixed factory. Sales is tied to a fixed set of customers. Responsibility does not overlap.

FAE is split into hardware and software

Hardware covers power, batteries, and semiconductor devices. Software covers module drivers and communication protocols. The lab, RMA, and technical documents are run by the same group.

Materials, warehouse, and shipping

Purchasing, part numbers, temperature- and humidity-controlled storage, lot traceability, import and export customs, and the hub warehouse all follow ISO procedures. When something goes wrong, the source can be found.

Administration and finance

The quality system, training, contracts and credit, expenses and tax. Work that does not create an order directly is what decides whether the company can be trusted for a long time.
