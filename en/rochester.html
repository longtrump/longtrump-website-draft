Enter a full part number, or try a prefix first

Results use 16 fields: part number, ECCN, RoHS, six price breaks, manufacturer code, Pb-free, total stock, date-code year, notes, and the quantity of that lot. Each row is one date code. The same part number appears on more than one row.

  • Prefix: LM358 lists 22 part numbers and 82 date-code rows.
  • Full part number: LM358ADR2G leaves a single part number.
  • If it is not found: send us the part number and sales will look up an alternate.

A blank cell means that quantity break does not apply. For “Prices: Over”, contact sales. Date code shows the last two digits of the year.
