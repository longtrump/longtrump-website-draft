A distributor’s real job
is making a spec buildable.

Long Trump is a technical distributor focused on electronic components. The lines span wireless modules, automotive and industrial sensing, power and analog semiconductors, high-speed optical interfaces, batteries, and end-of-life and long-lifecycle parts.

We do not resell inventory, and we do not only send a quote. Selection, samples, design review, compliance documents, production lead time — each step has a named owner and a record. When a part fails, the customer needs an answer, not a handoff.

Group sites cover Taipei, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hong Kong, and Japan, serving global ODM/OEM and brand customers.

Four things
that get a part onto your line.

Selection and design-in

From a spec comparison and alternate suggestions to sample requests, the PM and FAE sit in the customer’s development meeting — not only when a shortage shows up.

  • Spec comparison and pin-to-pin alternate suggestions
  • Samples, EVKs, and factory technical documents
  • Design review and introduction schedule control

Applications engineering (FAE)

Hardware and software FAEs handle measured problems in power, batteries, RF, and sensors. Complaints follow an RMA analysis process, and the reply is based on evidence.

  • Lab measurement, debug, and waveform analysis
  • RMA failure analysis and factory feedback
  • Annual training and technical sharing

Inventory and delivery

The Taipei warehouse and overseas hub warehouses run together: temperature and humidity records, lot traceability, consignment, and VMI, set to the customer’s line rhythm rather than to contract clauses.

  • Safety-stock planning and lead-time commitments
  • Hub warehouse, consignment, and VMI
  • Temperature and humidity monitoring, and lot traceability

Compliance and risk

An ISO 9001:2015 quality system, supplier evaluation, end-use statements for export control, and shelf-life control. The file is there when it is needed.

  • ISO 9001 procedures and internal audits
  • Export control (EUS) and factory authorization documents
  • Supplier evaluation, CoC, and shelf life

Quality
is a document you can audit.

Our quality policy is only six characters in Chinese: Learn, Innovate, Perform. They are not a slogan. At every management review, internal audit, and external assessment, we have to produce evidence for these three things.

Learn

Each year we schedule internal training, and turn factory technology, market change, and customer experience into knowledge the team shares.

Innovate

From selection advice to process automation, improvement is not a project. It is part of the daily work.

Perform

Lead time, inventory turns, customer satisfaction — each has a target, a tracker, and a review.
